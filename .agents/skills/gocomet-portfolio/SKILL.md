## 5. Implemented Visual System & Interactive Components

### Home Page & 3D Interactive Hero (`src/pages/HomePage.jsx`)
* **Viewport Lock**: Exactly `100vw` by `100vh` without scrollbars (`document.body.style.overflow = 'hidden'`).
* **Header Bar**:
  * Home Page: Center-aligned branding bar (`Deepak S` | `Portfolio for GoComet — Senior Brand Designer Role` + `AI-First Designer` sparkles tag), balanced cleanly above the 3D globe hero with no extraneous anchor links.
  * Inner Showcase Pages (`Graphic Design`, `Websites`, `Motion`): Left side maintains consistent branding (`Deepak S` anchor button | role title + AI tag); right side features exclusively the `[← Home]` button to drive reviewers back to the interactive 3D globe hero.

### Deep Ocean & Maritime Freight Layer (`src/components/OceanBackground.jsx`)
* **Context**: Honors GoComet's core freight visibility and container tracking business.
* **Implementation**:
  * 3 undulating SVG/CSS waves with realistic maritime gradient depths (`#040a18`, `#06132e`, `#0a1c44`).
  * Underwater caustics animation with subtle drifting luminescents.
  * **3 Staggered Container Vessels**:
    1. *GoComet Blue Vessel*: Lower hull, bridge tower with radar mast, GoComet blue container stacks.
    2. *Marine Emerald Vessel*: Mid-distance sailing cargo carrier.
    3. *MSC Violet / Orange Vessel*: Deep background cargo carrier.
  * Staggered travel durations (34s, 44s, 54s) ensuring at least one container vessel is visible sailing in the ocean at all times.

### Three.js WebGL 3D Earth Globe (`src/components/InteractiveGlobe.jsx`)
* **Scene Setup**:
  * Perspective Camera: 45° FOV at `(0, 0, 13.5)`.
  * Globe Geometry: Sphere of radius `8.6`, lowered position `globePositionY = -8.75` (lowered from -8.1 for spacious top breathing room), natural tilt `0.14` rad.
* **Custom Shaders & Materials**:
  * Continents: Matte corporate navy (`#0e224e`) mapped via NASA earth texture.
  * Oceans: Deep GoComet blue (`#061a48` to `#004fe6`) masked with softened specular map (`smoothstep(0.18, 0.40)` to prevent noise).
  * Atmosphere: Cyan Fresnel glow (`#00e5ff`) with outer shell at `radius * 1.018`.
* **Logistics Arcs & 3D Cargo Ships**:
  * 3 Trans-Oceanic Routes: Shanghai ⇄ Los Angeles, Singapore ⇄ Rotterdam, Santos ⇄ Rotterdam.
  * 3D Ships: Miniature cargo ships with pointed hull, bridge cabin, colored container stacks, and glowing green mast navigation lights sailing along 3D Catmull-Rom splines at a serene speed of `0.0006`.
* **Exact Ray-Sphere Tangency Algorithm**:
  * Uses binary search ray-sphere tangency calculation from camera position through screen X fractions to compute the exact sub-pixel screen `(x, y)` coordinate where camera rays graze the outer atmospheric rim.
  * Recalculates dynamically on window resize events with updated camera aspect, projection matrices, and world matrices.

### Discipline Beacon Cards & Laser Tethers (`src/components/GlobeBeacons.jsx`)
* **Card Rhythms & Dimensions**:
  * 3 Disciplines:
    1. **Graphic Design** (Left, horizon X: 26%, width: 320px, tether offset: 48px)
    2. **Websites • Landing Pages • AI** (Center, horizon X: 50%, widened to 420px, single-line title, tether offset: 62px, featured AI violet border/glow)
    3. **Motion** (Right, horizon X: 74%, width: 320px, tether offset: 48px)
  * Styling (`src/App.css`): Center card widened to `420px` with `white-space: nowrap` for clean single-line typography; side cards at `320px`; unified GoComet Electric Blue (`#0054ff`) pill button (`Explore Work ↗`).
  * Micro-Motion: `@keyframes float-subtle` (`0px` to `-3px`), keeping cards gently breathing while firmly grounded on the globe.
* **Vertical Laser Tether Lines**:
  * Dual-layer laser line with travelling photon animation (`tether-photon`), descending from the bottom of each card directly into the glowing contact node on the globe rim.
  * Contact Node: White `contact-dot` with dual cyan ping rings and a radiant halo positioned squarely on the illuminated cyan atmospheric rim of the globe.

### Footer & Resume Integration (`src/components/Footer.jsx`)
* **Ribbon Bar**: Fixed bottom footer (min-height `74px`, padding `24px 48px`, font size `0.95rem`).
* **Resume Access**: "View Resume" button links directly to `/Deepak_S_Senior_Visual_Designer.pdf` (placed in `public/` and `assets/`).
