About the job

What We're Looking For

* 5+ years of professional experience in Graphic Design.  
* Strong portfolio showcasing branding, digital design, website design, presentations and marketing collateral.  
* Expert proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign).  
* Strong expertise in Figma.  
* Hands-on experience with motion graphics and video editing using Adobe After Effects and Premiere Pro (or equivalent tools).  
* Experience designing modern B2B SaaS websites and landing pages.  
* Understanding of responsive web design and user experience principles.  
* Excellent typography, layout, colour theory and visual storytelling skills.  
* Ability to translate complex product concepts into clean, engaging visuals.  
* Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate across cross-functional teams.  
* High ownership, attention to detail and ability to work in a fast-paced environment.

AI-First Expectations Design at GoComet is AI-enabled by default.

* We're looking for someone who actively uses modern AI tools to improve creativity and speed—not just generate images.

You Should Be Comfortable

* Using AI tools for concept generation, image creation, video generation, editing and creative exploration.  
* Leveraging AI to accelerate repetitive design tasks while maintaining high creative quality.  
* Designing and shipping website pages using AI-assisted design and modern visual development workflows.  
* Experimenting with emerging AI design tools and recommending better ways of working.  
* Using AI to improve productivity without compromising brand consistency or design quality.
